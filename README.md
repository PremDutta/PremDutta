### What I'm building in the open

A config-driven router that picks the right AI video model for every request, fails over when a vendor goes down, stops when a request can't succeed anywhere, and never bills a user for a vendor's outage. Runnable in seconds, fully tested, with an interactive playground.

### How I work

- **Unit economics first.** Every model choice is also a margin choice. I track cost per generation, retake rate and failover cost the way other teams track uptime.
- **Build vs buy, honestly.** Self-hosting open-source models made a real free tier possible. Hiring for deployment skill beat hiring a research team.
- **Governance unblocks revenue.** Designing a likeness and rights flow for real people is what closed our anchor enterprise contract.
- **Packaging is config, not code.** Pricing experiments shouldn't wait on an engineering sprint.
